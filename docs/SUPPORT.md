# BottleShelf Support

BottleShelf is a local-first iOS app for managing beauty product expiry dates, inventory, and reminders.

## Get Help

For support, bug reports, or feature requests, open an issue here:

https://github.com/AutumnFarmer/BottleShelf/issues

## Common Notes

- BottleShelf does not require an account.
- Product records and selected photos are stored locally on your device.
- Expiry reminders use iOS local notifications.
- BottleShelf Pro purchases and refunds are handled by Apple through the App Store.
