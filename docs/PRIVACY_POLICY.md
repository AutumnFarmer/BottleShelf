# BottleShelf Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-21

BottleShelf is a local-first iOS app for tracking beauty products, expiry dates, storage locations, and reminders.

## Data We Do Not Collect

BottleShelf does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal data with the developer.

The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, or a developer-operated backend server in version 1.0.

## Data Stored On Your Device

The following information may be saved locally on your device when you use the app:

- Product names, brands, categories, dates, status, notes, and storage locations.
- Product photos that you choose from your photo library.
- Local reminder settings and notification schedules.

This information stays on your device unless you choose to delete the app, delete individual records, or use system-level device backup features controlled by Apple.

## Photos

BottleShelf requests photo library access only when you choose to attach a product photo. Selected photos are used inside the app and are stored locally.

## Notifications

BottleShelf uses local notifications to remind you about product expiry dates. Notification permission is controlled by iOS and can be changed in system settings.

## Purchases

BottleShelf Pro is purchased through Apple In-App Purchase. Payment, purchase history, refunds, and subscription or purchase account handling are managed by Apple. BottleShelf only checks purchase entitlement through Apple's StoreKit APIs.

## Children

BottleShelf is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
